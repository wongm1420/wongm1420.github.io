Sep 2023 – Present · Zester Limited

Zester Limited

Co-founder, COO & CFO

What it was

Zester teaches the UN Sustainable Development Goals through a card game called Chief Zester of Earth. It grew out of a school project at Island School into a full social enterprise, now with a digital learning platform (ZestLabs) alongside the card game and in-person workshops for schools and corporates.

My role

Co-founder, COO & CFO. I run day-to-day operations and partner relationships on the COO side, and bookkeeping, tax filings, and sponsorship budgeting on the CFO side.

What I produced

Built out our school and corporate network to 44+ schools and partners including HSBC, BNY, and TTI, delivered 6 sustainability workshops across Hong Kong and London, and ran the SDG Innovation League, an inter-school competition, from the ground up.

What came of it

30,000+ students reached, with 2,000+ active on ZestLabs. Zester won JA Asia Pacific Company of the Year, 1st out of 81,000+ students, and placed in the top 6 globally out of 32,000 teams at the De La Vega Global Entrepreneurship Awards.

Market Strategy & Expansion: Women's Premium Activewear Brand

Project Consultant

My role

Project Consultant, leading market sizing, competitor benchmarking, customer research, and sales-channel analysis.

Apr 2026 – Present · Liber Research Community

Cross-City Comparative Study on Job Vacancy Trends

Researcher (Volunteer)

What it was

A cross-city comparative study on job vacancy trends for Liber Research Community, an independent Hong Kong policy think tank, looking at whether Hong Kong's declining job openings are a local labour market issue or part of a broader global pattern.

My role

Researcher (Volunteer), sourcing and analysing the data and writing up the findings for a policy audience.

What I produced

Pulled together around 10 public datasets into a spreadsheet model comparing unemployment-to-vacancy ratios across major global cities, then wrote the analysis up for publication.

What came of it

Published across 3 channels, reaching 4,000+ engagements and 100K+ overall reach, and later cited in an HK01 article on Hong Kong's labour market.
